HOW IT WORKS

From your profile to a stronger interview

Five connected modules guide the practice session, explain feedback, and help you identify what to work on next.

01

Candidate Profile & Question Planning

Upload your resume and connect public GitHub project evidence. The system uses relevant information to plan role-specific questions instead of relying only on generic trivia.

Profile · Resume · Projects
02

Interview Room & Answer Evaluation

Answer timed questions and receive structured feedback across technical correctness, depth, reasoning, relevance, and clarity. Answer validation helps catch empty, too-short, or repeated responses.

Practice · Validate · Evaluate
03

Integrity Intelligence

Transparent browser-event logging can provide context for human review. A browser event alone is not proof of misconduct and does not automatically disqualify a candidate.

Transparent · Contextual
04

Resume-to-Interview Skill Mapping

Compare skills claimed on a resume, available project evidence, and skills demonstrated in answers. Missing evidence is marked as not yet assessed—not automatically treated as failure.

Claimed skills · Evidence · Demonstration
05

Explainable Reports & Improvement Plan

Review evidence-backed feedback, skill gaps, and recommended learning steps. Reports support people making decisions; they do not make autonomous hiring decisions.

Reports · Learning · Human review
